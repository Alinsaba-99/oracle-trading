F.A.Q.

Questions,answered

Everything you need to know about E8 One before you start.

It is a fully customizable 1‑step challenge, with NO payout caps or buffers designed for those who are looking to trade Forex, Indices, commodities, or Crypto without any payout limitations.
With the On-demand feature, the earliest you can request a payout is 3 days after starting trading in the Performance stage, as long as all requirements are fulfilled.
Traditional Trailing Drawdown moves with the trader's equity; however, our Dynamic Drawdown loss level will only increase when you are closing profits. Meaning, we count what you keep, not what you almost had. Swing traders are not punished for floating P&L while they hold a profitable trade.
There is no consistency rule in the challenge phase, but in the performance stage, there is a 40% best day rule. With a 40% Best Day Rule, you must ensure that no single trading day exceeds more than 40% of your total generated profits.
In the challenge stage (Phase-1), there are no profit caps or consistency rules, meaning you can pass your challenge in one day.
