E8 is a SimFi (Simulated Finance) platform where traders develop and demonstrate their skills using simulated capital. What am I risking?
Only the challenge fee. There are no deposits, no margin requirements, and no personal capital deployed on your behalf. The environment is simulated - the rules and market data are not.

A device, an internet connection, and a trading strategy. Select your account size, complete KYC, and you are ready to begin the SimFi Challenge.

Pass the SimFi Challenge by hitting the profit target while staying within the guardrails. Once you reach the SimFi Performance Account stage and meet the payout conditions, you can request payouts based on your validated performance data.
