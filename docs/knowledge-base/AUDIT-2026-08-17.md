## 10 paper mancanti più importanti (cross-domain)

### BL-KB-99: Bailey-Lopez de Prado 2018 — Haircut Sharpe Ratio
- **Perché**: critico per validazione Oracle. `Sharpe_haircut = Sharpe × (1 - haircut)` dove haircut depende da N trials + skew/kurt. Da implementare PRIMA del DSR calc.
- **Status**: NON studiato. Audit critico BL-KB-99.
- **Effort**: ~1 giorno per paper review + formula.

### BL-KB-100: Kyle 1985 — "Continuous Auctions and Insider Trading"
- **Perché**: fondamento microstruttura. λ (price impact parameter), insider + noise trader dynamics. Manca in dominio 04.
- **Status**: NON studiato. Audit critico BL-KB-100.

### BL-KB-101: Almgren-Chriss 2001 — "Optimal Execution of Portfolio Transactions"
- **Perché**: execution layer. Permanent + temporary market impact. Half-life of trades. Critical per Oracle paper broker simulation.
- **Status**: NON studiato. Audit critico BL-KB-101.

### BL-KB-102: Easley-Lopez de Prado-O'Hara 2012 — VPIN (Volume-Synchronized Probability of Informed Trading)
- **Perché**: **PROXY ORDER FLOW DA L1 TRADE DATA** — sblocca parzialmente hard-block US equities L2! Usa tick rule + bulk volume classification.
- **Status**: NON studiato. Game-changer per dominio 04.

### BL-KB-103: Asness-Moskowitz-Pedersen 2013 — "Value and Momentum Everywhere"
- **Perché**: 8 asset classes, value + momentum premia consistent. Common global risk + liquidity link. Manca in dominio 01 + 08.
- **Status**: NON studiato. Critico per ensemble multi-asset.

### BL-KB-104: Lo 2017 — "Adaptive Markets Hypothesis"
- **Perché**: framework moderno meta-synthesis. Sostituisce Hamilton HMM con evolutionary dynamics. Behavioral + EMH reconciliation.
- **Status**: NON studiato. Critico per dominio 13.

### BL-KB-105: Daniel-Hirshleifer-Subrahmanyam 1998 — "Investor Psychology and Security Market Under/Overreactions"
- **Perché**: CORE behavioral finance. Overconfidence + biased self-attribution. Manca in dominio 12.
- **Status**: NON studiato.

### BL-KB-106: Hong-Stein 2003 — "Differences of Opinion, Short-Sale Constraints and Market Crashes"
- **Perché**: disagreement + bubbles. Critical per panic detection.
- **Status**: NON studiato.

### BL-KB-107: Hou-Xue-Zhang 2015 — "Digesting Anomalies" (q5-factor model originale)
- **Perché**: validation framework per factor filter. q5 model subsumes many anomalies.
- **Status**: NON studiato. Critico per dominio 01 capability map.

### BL-KB-108: Goyal-Welch 2008 — "Horse Race" equity premium prediction
- **Perché**: 14 macroeconomic predictors benchmark. R² OOS basso → realtà check su macro factor predictability.
- **Status**: NON studiato. Critico per dominio 02 edge.

### BL-KB-109: Lopez de Prado 2019 — "Tactical Investment Algorithms" + Crowded Strategies
- **Perché**: strategy selection bias detection. Critical per Oracle 11-lane ensemble (potenzialmente correlate).
- **Status**: NON studiato. Critico per dominio 13.

### BL-KB-110: Loughran-McDonald 2011 — Financial dictionary (vs Harvard IV-4 Tetlock)
- **Perché**: 6 categories (negative, positive, uncertainty, litigious, modal, Harvard IV). Meglio di Tetlock per financial disclosures.
- **Status**: NON studiato. Audit BL-KB-110.

- **Implementation**: `analytics/validation/haircut_sharpe.py`

- `MinTRL = 1 + (1 - γ3×SR + (γ4-1)/4 × SR²) × ln((1 - conf)/(1 - Φ(SR × sqrt(T-1))))²`

- **Implementation**: integrate in `dsr.py` (BL-KB-20)
