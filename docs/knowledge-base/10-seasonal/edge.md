## Verdetto edge

**Edge forte maintained**:
- **Halloween** (Bouman-Jacobsen 2002) — 323y data, 89/114 countries. Robust globally.
- **Santa Claus rally** — 76% positive since 1950, +1.3% avg.
- **Turn of month** — concentrated returns, institutional + salary inflows.

**Edge decayed**:
- January effect (post-1980s) → marginal +0.5-1%.
- Day of week (post-1990s) → near zero.

**Edge too small sample**:
- Presidential cycle — 18 cycles only.
- Holiday effect — marginal.

## Regime dipendenza

- Halloween: regime-independent (robust across centuries + countries).
- TOM: persistent institutional inflows, regime-dep via sentiment.
- Santa Claus: depends on prior year sentiment (stronger after weak years).

## Cost-realism check

- **Trading costs**: seasonal low-frequency, ~4-12 trades/yr → marginal.
- **Tax**: ETFs tax-efficient.
- **Net edge realistico**: +1-2%/yr post-cost su 5y. Sharpe 0.3-0.5.
