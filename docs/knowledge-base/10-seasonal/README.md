# Dominio 10 — Seasonal / calendar patterns

> Knowledge base Oracle — studio approfondito 2026-08-17 via Tavily API.

## Sintesi esecutiva

L'analisi stagionale ha **edge documentato** ma con decay post-publication:

1. **Halloween / "Sell in May" effect** (Bouman-Jacobsen 2002): winter returns (Nov-Apr) 4.2% higher than summer (May-Oct). 89/114 countries show effect, 42 statistically significant. 323 years of data — robust globally.
2. **January effect** (Keim 1983): small-cap stocks outperform in January. Tax-loss selling + reinvestment. **Decayed post-1980s** when widely known.
3. **Santa Claus rally**: last 5 trading days of Dec + first 2 of Jan. S&P 500 +1.3% average since 1950. 76% of time positive. Lighter volume + January effect anticipation.
4. **Turn of month effect** (Hensel-Ziemba 1987): first 4 trading days of month concentrated returns. DJIA +0.473% in TOM vs +0.349% total monthly average → ROM negative.
5. **Day of week effect** (French 1980): Monday lower returns, Friday higher. Settle over 3 calendar days. **Decayed post-1990s**.

**Cap to build Oracle**:
1. Halloween signal (Nov-Apr long bias)
2. Santa Claus rally signal (last 5 Dec + first 2 Jan)
3. Turn of month signal (first 4 days of month)
4. Calendar anomaly composite
